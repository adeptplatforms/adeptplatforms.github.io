Why we built adept

Let’s go back to treating doctors like people.

Education leaders spend too much time looking at names on spreadsheets, inboxes and shared drives. The hours spent reconciling rotas, chasing forms and copying data between them are hours not spent on the people the programme exists for.

adept takes that administrative weight off educators, so trainees are met as names and faces, supported through every rotation, not managed as rows in a sheet.

Omar Jundi, founder. Consultant anaesthetist and intensivist, NHS Clinical Entrepreneur. LinkedIn

The programme’s numbers, without the spreadsheet.

The figures a Head of School, a quality visit or the dean’s report asks for come straight from the records directors already keep, so they are ready when asked and they agree with the plan.

  • An annual programme report. The training year as a PDF, with a spreadsheet of the same figures. It names no one.
  • Proof of Code of Practice notice. When each trust and trainee was told, and how long joiners waited for a placement.
  • Every report exports as a spreadsheet or PDF.

Built around the people who run training.

Training programme directors

Plan rotations and placements, oversee the whole cohort, run ARCPs, decide working-pattern requests, and report to the Head of School, all from one view of the year.

College tutors

See who is arriving at your trust and when, oversee sites, modules and supervisors, and keep the local handbook (rota, contacts, induction) ready before day one.

Trainees

See your placement plan and progression on your phone, ask for a change of working pattern, check in to teaching and add your dates to your calendar.
